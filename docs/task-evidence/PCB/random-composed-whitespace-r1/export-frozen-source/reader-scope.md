# Independent reader scope: first-random composed board

This reader qualifies only the accepted public request
`tsj-alpha/6/MEDIUM/COMPOSED_CONTROLLED_INDICATOR/-2750102463469069236`.
It does not regenerate or choose a board. The task adapter must construct the
ordinary selected request, pass normal physical admission, preserve geometry,
graph, endpoint, route-quality, clearance, access and identity checks, and dispose
the actual owner through its normal cleanup path.

`qualify.py` loads the published
`sparse-board-diagnostic-r1/qualify.py` only after checking its frozen
`baseline-inputs.json` SHA-256. Its in-memory source transformation is limited to
two guarded literals: the `EXPECTED` identity is narrowed to the actual composed
request, and `placementPackingVersion==3` becomes `==4`. The original
`validate_row` implementation then checks the complete inventory, endpoints,
graph/geometry immutability, independent area/copper/via/protected-void metrics,
grid-10 whitespace sampler, strict packing predicates, and canonical packing
receipt. The reader leaves the `normal-packing@3` receipt epoch unchanged; it
does not reinterpret that record as the layout constraint version.

Invoke the independent qualifier with the exact private adapter path:

```text
python -B qualify.py --private-adapter-path <private-adapter>
```

Before accepting evidence, it calls `export_inputs.record(adapter)` and compares
the returned seal to `export-inputs.json`. Both `canaries` and `composed` phases
must have successful actual tool exits, distinct post-exit cleanup receipts,
matching two-driver birth records, and the exact before/after input-audit marker.
The baseline six-field execution projection must also match the before and after
observations exactly.
The canary source and results must show a leading-zero signed seed rejected at
the selected adapter's parameter binding and the excluded `RC_DELAY` family
rejected there with an otherwise valid composed seed; no compiler or native
export may occur for either negative.

The composed receipt must contain one diagnostic row and one independent
whitespace-sampler row, the exact composed native completion marker, and the
maintained one-suite verifier marker. The original row reader must accept the
row. Five nonvacuous mutations must fail at their intended original assertions:
public identity, pad/net membership, independent copper, independent protected
void, and obsolete layout packing version 3.

The accepted baseline, player, build and reviewed board-surface inputs are reused
only through the pinned seal. The separate board-surface review remains
`NO_GO` for another paint change; the qualifier rechecks the consumed source and
image hashes. Any resulting assessment is limited to this finite diagnostic and
the observed board geometry and packing receipt. Free whitespace cells do not
prove that a legal placement exists. No production, browser, GWT, all-seed,
manufacturing, optimality, or new player-flow claim is made here.
