## What the callback can and cannot supply

The census observer exists only in the packet's COPY snapshot (`docs/task-evidence/PCB/routed-passive-leg-census-r1/source/src/com/lushprojects/circuitjs1/client/SeededPcbLayoutGenerator.java:22-46,611-626`), not in current production `src`. It observes the finalized routed `PcbBoardLayout` after `finishRoutedPlan` has placed labels, compacted the board, positioned the tray, optionally enlarged the canvas, and passed placement and geometry validation (`src/com/lushprojects/circuitjs1/client/SeededPcbLayoutGenerator.java:877-938`). The callback carries the board, attempt, score/quality, area, and layout, but not the `PcbPlacementPlanner.Plan`.

Do not fabricate an “exact Plan.” `Plan` also owns `courtyardArea`, `demandArea`, `candidate`, and `evaluations`, which the callback does not provide; `Plan.materialize()` synthesizes canvas/tray dimensions and materializes only footprints and regions, omitting finalized traces, holes, labels, routing statistics, and crop (`PcbPlacementPlanner.java:30-50`). The maintained placement validator accepts a `Vector<PcbFootprint>` directly, so reconstruct that finalized footprint projection instead (`PcbPlacementPlanner.java:1038-1053`).

## Validation and independent proof

Run exactly once, in this order, without a catch that turns rejection into success:

1. Rebuild footprints from the candidate layout and call `PcbPlacementPlanner.validate(board, board.getPlacementConstraints(), candidate.getBoardOutline(), candidateFootprints)`. This checks constrained envelope/domain/corridor placement and global access; `PcbAccessPlanner` caps its grid at 700,000 cells and calls `GenerationWorkScope.check()` during the flood fill (`PcbPlacementPlanner.java:1038-1053`; `PcbAccessPlanner.java:12-44`).
2. Call `candidate.validateGeometry(board)`. This supplies full layout/route geometry, endpoint-to-pad agreement, hole and courtyard checks, silkscreen, route quality, clearance, and a rebuilt full-contact conductor graph with pristine-net connectivity (`PcbBoardLayout.java:196-203,248-295,566-568,574-620`; `PcbConductorBuilder.java:140-189,261-329`). It detects new segment/segment and segment/land contacts, including changed crossings, overlaps, pad/via touches, and shorts. Because these contacts are implicit, matching explicit endpoint IDs alone is insufficient.
3. For the P07 trial, also call `new PcbTwoLayerRules(board,candidate).validate(candidate)`. `validateGeometry` invokes this rule only when the board declares barriers; the explicit call ensures P07 domain/body/drill checks also run when that barrier list is empty (`PcbBoardLayout.java:290-295`; `PcbTwoLayerRules.java:332-378`).
4. Independently capture baseline and candidate graphs. Compare `pristine().padsConnected(a,b)` for every pair of sorted stable board pad IDs. Do not compare builder junction/edge serial IDs or canonical graph text: coordinates and implicit contacts can renumber them. Separately map traces by unique `sourceId`; require the same complete source-ID set and exact `netId`, start/end pad IDs, layer, exposure, and width. Require all non-moved path vertices to match exactly and each moved endpoint to equal its candidate pad. Union `Edge.getSources()` across graph edges of kind `TRACE` and require that union to equal the route source-ID set (`PcbConductorGraph.java:42-68,192-194,264-279`). The geometry validator's graph rebuild separately rejects unrelated-net copper contact.

The proof has one binary outcome: all these checks pass and the candidate is recorded as a valid private geometry trial, or the first construction/validation/comparison failure escapes as a failed proof. Do not catch a generic `IllegalStateException`, choose a fallback move, call the router, update the winner, or change selection/budget policy. `GenerationWorkScope.check()` delegates to the active `GenerationJob.checkpoint()` and propagates cancellation, stale-owner, and deadline failures (`GenerationWorkScope.java:8-19`; `GenerationJob.java:97-115,354-369`). Root 5 remains a no-move control and must retain its baseline geometry/graph hashes. No physical improvement is established by the analytic `-10`; the local move does not shrink the outline or trigger a winner decision.
