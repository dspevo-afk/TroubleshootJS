# Qualification criteria frozen before the new holdout

Population and numerical limits are those declared in PLAN.md. The 24 development
and 48 holdout signed-long seeds are independently reproducible SplitMix64 vectors.
Every requested case stays in the evidence, including explicit rejection, timeout
or exhausted work. No failed candidate is a playable success.

RB15_CONTROL/EASY is the only broadly qualified family/profile. Fixed-layout
reference families stay visibly curated. Normal launches use at most four distinct
ordinal candidates and share the original 90-second/640-unit/five-second-unit
budgets. Exact replay has one candidate. All normal physical/electrical/diagnostic
and repair checks remain mandatory; all three hypotheses remain in each board.

Holdout acceptance: at least 24 of 48 pass the physical bounds and at least 24
pass actual compiled normal admission. Admitted layouts must have at least 12
macro groups and three aspect bands; no group exceeds 25% of admitted holdout.
Groups are deterministic in predeclared seed order. Two layouts are near-clones
when fewer than max(2, floor(common component count / 4)) common component origins
move by at least 8% of their respective board width or height. Translation is
removed; comparison uses exact integer cross-products, not value/fault labels or
raw geometry hashes. Aspect bands are nearest 0.2 width/height intervals.

Compiled boards must match the native discrete design, dimensions and every
component position. Full signed seeds and accepted current-epoch replay are exact.
Real routing and mid-proof cancellation restore the prior owner; a genuine
out-of-envelope candidate must reject before a later ordinal candidate succeeds.
Unexpected programming/cleanup failures stop qualification. Two isolated browser
owners may evaluate independent cases concurrently; candidates inside each launch
remain serial. Timings are qualification observations, not single-browser FPS.
